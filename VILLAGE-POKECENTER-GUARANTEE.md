# Village PokeCenter Guarantee 1.20.1

Forge 47.4.22 + Generations Structures 1.0.0.

Behavior:
- Plains village: forces a Generations village PokéCenter path.
- Desert village: forces a Generations village PokéCenter path.
- Retries on later eligible street branches until the PokéCenter building pool is reached.
- PokéMart: one 50% decision per village, maximum one forced attempt.
- Savanna/Taiga/Snowy are intentionally untouched because Generations Structures 1.0.0 does not enable village PokéCenter pools for them.

Only newly generated villages/chunks are affected.
